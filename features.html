Everything Aniko does

One app for your library, your list and every screen at home. Here's all of it.

Library Watching AniList Your devices Home server Watch order and playlists Manga Parental controls Themes Extensions And the rest

Library

Point Aniko at your folders once. It does the sorting.

  • Matched by itselfReads every file name and matches it to AniList: seasons, parts, sequels, absolute numbering, movies, OVAs and specials.
  • Every video formatmkv, mp4, avi, webm, mov, ts and the rest.
  • Keeps itself up to dateWatches your folders and picks up new files as they appear.
  • Local library pageOnly what's on your disks: recently added, filters by type and status, grid or list, sizes, resolutions and release groups.
  • Artwork that works offlineCovers, banners, descriptions, episode titles and thumbnails are saved on your computer.
  • Safe with removable drivesUnplug a drive and its matches are still there when it comes back.
  • Tools to fix thingsCorrect a match by hand, change episode numbers, lock files, ignore junk.
  • Clean upSee how full the drive is, find episodes you have twice, and put watched ones in a trash you can undo for 30 days.

Watching

Two players, one memory.

  • Built-in player or mpvUse Aniko's own player, or mpv if you already love it. Aniko controls both.
  • Plays what your device can'tFiles a device can't play are converted while you watch, with your graphics card when it has one.
  • Resume where you stoppedThe position is saved every few seconds.
  • Remembers your tracksYour audio and subtitle choice is kept per anime, even when the track order changes between episodes.
  • Skips the parts you skipOpenings, endings, recaps and previews, by themselves or with a button.
  • Up nextA card in the ending leads to the next episode, with auto play if you like.
  • Subtitles your waySize, background and position per device. Fix timing with z and x. Image subtitles work too.
  • Seek bar previewsSee where you're going before you get there.
  • Picture in pictureThe episode carries on in a small window above everything else.
  • Episodes at handPress E for a grid of episodes without leaving the player.

AniList

Your list, kept current without thinking about it.

  • Progress updates itselfAn episode counts once you've passed the point you set (85% by default).
  • Lists, scores and rewatchesEverything you'd do on AniList, from the app.
  • Discover, search and scheduleBrowse what's airing, search with filters, see this week's episodes.
  • Your profileYour numbers, favourites, and what the people you follow are watching.
  • Works without an accountThe list is kept on your computer instead.
  • Changes kept while offlineList changes made without a connection are sent when it's back.

Your devices

Every Aniko at home, working as one.

  • Library sharingAnikos on your network find each other. Share one computer's library with the rest; files play as if they were local.
  • A real TV appFor Fire TV and Android TV, built for the remote, with Android's own video player.
  • Continue watching, everywhereStop on one device and carry on from another.
  • One AniList loginShare your account with the TV instead of typing a password with a remote.
  • Wake on LANWake the computer that has the files from the TV. It stays awake while it's streaming.
  • From your phoneA phone layout in the browser, on your home network.
  • UploadsSend episodes or whole folders from one computer to another's library, carrying on after a dropped connection.
  • Same theme on all of themChange the colours once.

Home server

Add a Raspberry Pi and your library is always on.

  • mediaserverA small server for a computer that stays on. It shares its library and streams to every Aniko at home.
  • Converts on the PiIt converts what a device can't play, using the Pi's video chip.
  • Keeps devices in stepProgress, lists and theme, to the second.
  • A dashboard in the browserWhat's streaming to whom, the library, the drive and the Pi's health.
  • A Server page in the appStatus, load and drive space without opening the dashboard.
  • An audit logWho changed what, sign-ins and deletions.

Watch order and playlists

For series where the order isn't obvious.

  • Story order and release orderOn every anime's page, for its whole franchise.
  • Movies tabFilms that belong to the series, even loosely.
  • Recaps and side stories markedSpin-offs are kept apart.
  • Filler tagsFiller episodes are labelled in the episode list.
  • Your own orderRearrange it and Aniko keeps your version.
  • PlaylistsLine up episodes from different shows and play them through.

Manga

A reader for the manga you keep.

  • Read offlineDownloaded chapters open without a connection, from this device or your server.
  • Three ways to readLong strip, single pages or two-page spreads, left to right or right to left.
  • Tidy filesEach chapter is a .cbz with its details, each series a folder with its cover, so other readers understand them too.

Parental controls

Decide what each device may show.

  • Block 18+Also hides Ecchi and anything tagged as sexual content.
  • Hide genres or titlesOr turn it around and allow only the anime you choose.
  • Per device, with a PINSet from your computer or from the server's dashboard.
  • See what was watchedEverything a device has played.

Themes

Calm by default, playful when you want.

  • Eight colour themesAniko, Midnight, Forest, Sakura, Ember, Ocean, Dusk and true Black for OLED screens.
  • Any accent colourPick a swatch or your own.
  • Six holiday themesNew Year, Valentine's Day, cherry blossoms, summer festival, Halloween and Christmas, each with decorations and an animation. On by the date, always, or never.
  • Classic lookPrefer how Aniko looked before 2.0? It's one switch.

Extensions

Add what other people have made.

  • A marketplace in the appBrowse, install, update and remove community extensions and plugins.
  • Run in a sandboxExtensions get no access to your files, your programs or your home network.
  • Made by othersAniko ships with none. What you install is your choice.

And the rest

Small things you'd miss.

  • AlertsWhen a new episode of something you're watching airs or is ready.
  • What should I watch?A dice on Home picks from your list.
  • StatsYour anime in numbers, month by month.
  • Backup and restoreSettings, history, list and matches in one file.
  • Quick searchCtrl K from anywhere.
  • Runs in the backgroundKeep sharing with the TV after you close the window, if you want.
  • Updates itselfOne button on Home, with What's new afterwards. Betas only if you ask.
  • Discord presenceShow what you're watching, or don't.
